1. Overview & Platform Identity

Welcome to GetFood (operating on the FoodSphere multi-tenant unified aggregator platform). GetFood acts as a unified digital ecosystem providing food discovery, order aggregation, and delivery logistics for seven specialized culinary restaurant brands: Jushh PK, Tandoori Stop, GetAFomo, Seen Banao, Dine At Blue, SandMelts, and Birdman Foods.

This Privacy Policy outlines how GetFood ("we", "us", or "our") collects, processes, protects, and discloses personal data when you interact with our mobile application (iOS & Android) and our affiliated brand websites. We are committed to upholding individual privacy rights and maintaining robust transparency regarding data handling.

2. Information We Collect

We collect information necessary to deliver seamless food aggregation, precise order fulfillment, and loyalty reward services. This includes:

2.1 Personal Information Provided directly by You

  • Account Identity: Username, email address, phone number, and password credentials created upon user registration.
  • Delivery Address Data: Street address, apartment/suite number, district, city, sector, and saved location tags (e.g. Home, Work).
  • Communication Details: Records of customer support inquiries, feedback ratings, and delivery notes.
  • Guest User Data: Guest tracking tokens (UUIDs), guest phone numbers, and delivery locations required for guest checkout.

2.2 Information Collected Automatically

  • Device Identifier & Technical Logs: IP address, operating system version, unique device tokens, mobile network operator, and push notification tokens (Firebase Cloud Messaging).
  • App & Browsing Usage: Screen views, active sessions, restaurant brand interactions, search queries, cart additions, and checkout drop-off rates.

3. Geolocation & Delivery Location Practices

Location data is vital to the core service offered by GetFood. We process geographic coordinates (latitude and longitude) to deliver accurate service experiences.

📍 How Location Tracking Works for Delivery

When you grant location permissions, GetFood uses foreground GPS data to detect your delivery address, calculate exact Haversine distances to nearby restaurant branches, verify branch delivery radiuses (e.g. 5 km radius enforcement), and estimate accurate preparation and transit times.

3.1 Rider Location Sharing

During an active order, your delivery address and precise coordinates are shared strictly with the assigned branch delivery personnel (BranchRider) and logistics coordinators to facilitate swift and accurate order fulfillment.

3.2 Controlling Location Permissions

You can enable or disable location permissions at any time through your device settings. If location services are disabled, you can manually enter your delivery street address.

4. How We Use Your Information

We process personal data for the following legitimate business purposes:

  • Order Processing & Dispatch: Transmitting order details, selected menu items, dietary instructions, and delivery addresses to the relevant restaurant branch kitchen and delivery rider.
  • Order Status Notifications: Dispatching real-time transactional updates via Firebase Push Notifications, SMS, or Email (e.g. Order Received, Preparing, Out for Delivery).
  • Loyalty Rewards Management: Accurately calculating earn rates, tracking accrued loyalty balances, verifying coupon code applicability, and processing point redemptions.
  • Customer Care & Support: Resolving order disputes, delivery delays, refund queries, or technical glitches.
  • Platform Protection & Fraud Prevention: Detecting fraudulent orders, unauthorized price tampering, and automated scraping attempts.

5. Data Sharing & Multi-Tenant Access

Because GetFood operates as a multi-tenant platform, data is isolated per restaurant brand while shared securely under standardized infrastructure:

  • Restaurant Branch Managers: Branch administrators only receive order metadata, item choices, customer phone numbers, and delivery addresses required to fulfill orders assigned to their specific restaurant branch.
  • Delivery Personnel: Riders receive customer name, phone contact, and address/coordinates strictly while an order status is active ("Out for Delivery").
  • Infrastructure Partners: We engage trusted third-party providers including Google Firebase (FCM push alerts), Cloudflare (CDN security & hosting), and Heroku/Render (cloud server hosting). These entities operate under strict data protection obligations.
  • Legal Obligations: We may disclose data if required by applicable statutory laws, court orders, or law enforcement requests.

6. Payment Security & Processing

GetFood prioritizes payment safety for all customers across Pakistan and international jurisdictions.

  • Cash on Delivery (COD): Primary payment method. Customer pays physical cash directly to the delivery rider upon arrival. No credit card data is involved.
  • Online Card Payments (Stripe / PayFast): When paying digitally, payment transactions are encrypted and handled directly by PCI-DSS Level 1 compliant payment gateways (Stripe or PayFast).
🔒 Payment Card Security Guarantee

GetFood servers NEVER store, inspect, process, or transmit full credit/debit card numbers, CVV codes, or banking PINs. All payment transactions are tokenized directly through licensed payment gateways.

7. Loyalty Points Program Processing

Registered users automatically participate in the GetFood Loyalty Points Program. Point accrual and redemption are logged in your account record.

  • Points earned on completed orders are calculated atomically on our backend servers.
  • If an order is cancelled or refunded, any loyalty points redeemed for that transaction are automatically restored to the user's account balance, and any unearned points associated with the cancelled order are revoked.
  • Points are non-transferable between users and carry no cash value outside the GetFood platform.

8. Data Retention & Security Measures

We implement industry-standard security safeguards to protect your personal information against unauthorized access, loss, or alteration:

  • Encryption: All data transmitted between mobile devices, websites, and backend servers is encrypted using Transport Layer Security (TLS 1.3 / SSL).
  • Authentication: Secure JSON Web Token (JWT) architecture with guest UUID tracking token isolation.
  • Retention Policy: Customer account profiles are retained as long as the account remains active. Transaction and audit logs are retained for accounting and compliance purposes for up to 3 years.

9. Your Rights & Account Deletion

You possess full control over your personal data under applicable data privacy frameworks:

  • Access & Export: You may inspect your profile details and past order history directly in the GetFood mobile app.
  • Rectification: You may edit your username, email, phone number, and default delivery addresses via the app Profile screen.
  • Account Deletion: You have the right to permanently delete your account and associated personal data.

To request complete deletion of your account and personal records, please submit an email request to privacy@getfood.pk with your registered phone number or email address. Account deletion requests are fulfilled within 7 business days.

10. Children's Privacy

The GetFood platform is not intended for use by children under the age of 13 (or 16 depending on local jurisdiction). We do not knowingly collect personal data from minors. If we discover that a child has provided us with personal information, we immediately delete it from our systems.
