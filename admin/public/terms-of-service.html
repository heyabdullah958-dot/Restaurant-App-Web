4. Pricing, Item Availability & Operating Hours

4.1 Menu Prices & Verification

Prices listed on GetFood reflect the current menu prices established by each restaurant branch. All orders undergo server-side verification to validate prices, selected add-ons/modifiers, and promotional codes prior to order confirmation.

4.2 Out-of-Stock Items

Item availability is updated dynamically. If an item becomes unavailable at a specific branch, it will display an "OUT OF STOCK" indicator on the menu screen and cannot be added to cart.

4.3 Operating Hours Enforcement

Each branch operates within defined opening and closing hours. Cart additions and checkout are restricted when a branch status indicates Closed.

5. Delivery Rules & Distance Radius Restrictions

Delivery services are governed by strict geographic boundaries designed to preserve food quality and temperature:

🛵 Haversine Distance & Delivery Radius Limits

Each restaurant branch specifies a maximum delivery radius (e.g. 5.0 km). Order placement is validated using the mathematical Haversine distance algorithm based on your delivery coordinates. Orders exceeding the maximum branch radius will be blocked during checkout.

Users are solely responsible for providing an accurate street address and active telephone contact number. Delivery riders will make up to two attempts to contact you upon arrival. If a user is unreachable after 10 minutes, the order may be cancelled without refund eligibility.
