Frequently Asked Questions

Delivery & Dine-In Information

How long does delivery take?
Delivery typically takes 30 to 45 minutes depending on your distance from the selected branch outlet. Naans are packed in thermal heat-retention bags to stay piping hot.
What payment methods are supported?
We accept Cash on Delivery (COD) across all areas. You pay the rider upon inspecting your fresh food order.
Do your branches have family dine-in seating?
Yes! All 3 branches (Johar Town, Lake City, and Baghbanpura) offer dedicated, comfortable air-conditioned family dining sections with live clay tandoor viewing.
