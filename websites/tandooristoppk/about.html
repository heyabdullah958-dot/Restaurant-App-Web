Our Roots & Passion

The Sacred Dance of
Fire, Clay & Spices

In an era of microwave shortcuts and electric ovens, Tandoori Stop was founded on a simple, uncompromising premise: the clay tandoor is sacred.

True Pakistani tandoori flavor cannot be faked. It requires genuine lump charcoal burning at 800 degrees Fahrenheit inside hand-molded clay walls, infusing every skewer of chicken and every hand-stretched dough with a deep, natural, aromatic woodsmoke char.

The Culinary Standard

Our Four Golden Principles

🏺

The Mud Tandoor

We build and maintain traditional earthen tandoors made from special riverbed clay and straw, creating thermal equilibrium that bakes naans in under 90 seconds.

🪵

Lump Acacia Charcoal

Zero chemical briquettes or gas lines. We source natural hardwood Kikar (Acacia) charcoal, imparting clean, smoky essence without acrid fumes.

🧂

14 Hand-Ground Spices

Whole coriander, black cumin, green cardamom, and Kashmiri chilies roasted and ground daily. No commercial curry powders or artificial food dyes.

🧼

Exemplary Hygiene

Stainless steel prep stations, daily temperature audits, filtered water ice, and strict food handling protocols at Johar Town, Lake City, and Baghbanpura.
