Why Lahore Chooses Tandoori Stop

We honor the age-old tradition of lump-charcoal fired clay ovens. No industrial shortcuts — just pure heritage flavour.

🏺

800°F Clay Tandoor

Handcrafted mud tandoors fired with premium lump charcoal create an intense radiant crust that locks in juices.

🥩

100% Halal Prime Cuts

Fresh poultry delivered each dawn, hand-trimmed and marinated for 12 hours in our family blend of 14 roasted spices.

🫓

Hand-Stretched Naans

Our master bakers slap each dough ball directly against the burning clay walls, delivering blistered, airy naans.

🥘

Clay Pot Handis

Simmered in earthen clay handis with pure butter and rich cream to preserve the deep aromatics of royal desi cooking.
