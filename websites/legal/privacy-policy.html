Privacy Policy for GetFood

Last Updated: July 2026

At GetFood ("we", "our", or "us"), accessible via our mobile application and platform services, we prioritize the privacy of our customers. This Privacy Policy outlines the types of information we collect and how we handle, store, and protect it.

1. Information We Collect

  • Account & Order Information: Name, phone number, delivery address, and order items provided during account registration or checkout.
  • Location Data: Geolocation coordinates collected with your permission to identify nearby restaurant branches and compute precise delivery estimates.
  • Device Information: Device model, OS version, push notification tokens, and app usage logs for diagnostics and security enforcement.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To process, fulfill, and deliver food orders placed across our partner brands.
  • To dispatch delivery details to assigned branch riders via secure notification channels.
  • To calculate loyalty point rewards and allow promotional discounts.
  • To send real-time order status notifications (e.g. Received, Out for Delivery).

3. Data Sharing & Third Parties

We do not sell or rent personal information to third parties. We share data strictly required for fulfillment with:

  • Partner Restaurant Branches: To prepare and pack your order.
  • Assigned Delivery Riders: Delivery name, address, and phone number to execute delivery.
  • Infrastructure Providers: Secure cloud hosting (Heroku, Cloudflare) and push notification delivery (Google Firebase FCM).

4. Data Retention & Security

Your order history and account profile are stored using encrypted HTTPS transmission and restricted database credentials. We retain data as long as your account remains active or as needed to maintain legal order audit logs.

5. User Rights & Account Deletion

You have the right to request access to your personal data, opt out of push notifications, or request complete account deletion by contacting our support team at support@getfood.pk.
