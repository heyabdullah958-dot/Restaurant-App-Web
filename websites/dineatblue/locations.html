Dining & Delivery FAQs

How fresh is the seafood served?
Our whole lobsters, mud crabs, pomfret, and tiger prawns are sourced directly from sustainable fisheries and flown in fresh. We never serve re-frozen or chemically treated catch.
How does delivery work for hot steamed seafood?
Our platters are packed in heavy-duty food-grade thermal foil seal boxes that trap heat and aromatic steam for up to 45 minutes, ensuring your lobster and crab arrive piping hot at your dining table.
Is Counter Pickup free of charge?
Yes! Select "🛍️ Pickup" at checkout and your order will be prepared with zero delivery fees. Your food will be waiting fresh and hot at our MM Alam Road counter in 20–25 minutes.
Do you accept Cash on Delivery (COD)?
Yes! We accept Cash on Delivery across all serviceable areas in Lahore. You pay upon receiving your fresh seafood package.
