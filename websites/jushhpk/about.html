The Street Revolution

Food That Makes You
Go Shhh…

We started Jushh with one obsession: bring the raw energy of Istanbul and Beirut street rotisseries straight to the heart of Lahore.

No bland mayo wraps. No frozen processed patties. Just pure stacked meats rotating on vertical flames, shaved paper-thin per order, tossed over sizzling spiced fries or packed tightly into warm Lebanese pocket breads.

Our Uncompromising Rules

The Jushh Code

🔥

Live Sliced Doner

Our chicken and beef doner cones roast continuously throughout the day. Meat is carved fresh in front of you — never reheated or pre-shaved.

🧄

Authentic Garlic Toum

Traditional whipped garlic dip emulsified with pure lemon and sea salt. Creamy, pungent, and addictive without industrial emulsifiers.

🥔

Double-Fried Crunch

Our doner fries undergo a two-stage frying process, ensuring an ultra-crispy shell that holds up under hot roasted meat and rich melting sauces.

🥫

The Pull-Tab Can

Desserts should be fun and ultra-fresh. Our pull-tab cans lock in moisture, keeping our Lotus, Nutella, and Red Velvet cakes decadent till the last spoonful.
